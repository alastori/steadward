# Leader Profiles

## 1. Maren Stahl — Operations Lead

**Stats**: judgment 7, speed 5, reliability 9, adaptability 3, communication 6, riskTolerance 3
**Tendencies**: cautious, fatigueRate 0.8, trustGrowthRate 1.1
**Backstory**: Former logistics coordinator. Keeps systems running through sheer consistency. Hates surprises. Would rather do something slowly and correctly than fast and wrong.
**Voice**: Clipped, factual. Reports outcomes without embellishment. Gets quieter when stressed, not louder.
**Flaw**: Rigidity. Struggles when plans change. Will resist reallocation even when it's clearly necessary.

**Example lines**:
- Delegation success: "Supply chain is stable. Output is within expected range."
- Delegation partial: "We managed, but the schedule slipped. I should have flagged the bottleneck earlier."
- Delegation failure: "I followed the protocol. The protocol was wrong."
- Event response: "We can absorb this, but not if we also take on the northern route."

## 2. Kael Osei — External Affairs Lead

**Stats**: judgment 5, speed 7, reliability 4, adaptability 8, communication 9, riskTolerance 7
**Tendencies**: aggressive, fatigueRate 1.0, trustGrowthRate 1.3
**Backstory**: Former trader and negotiator. Builds connections easily. Promises more than he can deliver. Genuinely believes he can make everything work.
**Voice**: Warm, persuasive, slightly too optimistic. Uses "we" instead of "I." Makes problems sound smaller than they are.
**Flaw**: Overcommitting. Will take on more initiatives than he can handle and deliver below expectations.

**Example lines**:
- Delegation success: "They're on board. Took some convincing, but we've got a solid agreement."
- Delegation partial: "The deal went through, mostly. We had to give up more than I wanted."
- Delegation failure: "I thought I had them. I didn't. We need a different approach."
- Event response: "Let me talk to them first. I can smooth this over."

## 3. Dr. Fen Vasara — Research Lead

**Stats**: judgment 9, speed 3, reliability 7, adaptability 5, communication 4, riskTolerance 2
**Tendencies**: cautious, fatigueRate 0.9, trustGrowthRate 0.7
**Backstory**: Planetary geologist. Meticulous, evidence-driven, allergic to guesswork. Slow to trust, slower to recommend action without data.
**Voice**: Precise, measured. Uses qualifiers. "The data suggests..." not "I think..."
**Flaw**: Analysis paralysis. Will delay decisions waiting for data that doesn't exist yet.

**Example lines**:
- Delegation success: "Results are consistent with the model. I've documented the methodology."
- Delegation partial: "Preliminary findings are promising, but I need another week to confirm."
- Delegation failure: "The hypothesis was wrong. I should have tested the secondary variable first."
- Event response: "I need to assess the data before recommending action."

## 5. Lin Seo-yun — Generalist / Floater

**Stats**: judgment 6, speed 6, reliability 6, adaptability 9, communication 7, riskTolerance 5
**Tendencies**: balanced, fatigueRate 1.4, trustGrowthRate 1.0
**Backstory**: Former field coordinator. Can slot into any role competently. Never the best at anything, always good enough. Burns out faster than specialists because she's never doing her "own" work.
